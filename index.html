YOUR FIRST SHIFT

Keep it smooth.

01 · Take the wheel

W / ↑ accelerates. A D / ← → steer.
S / ↓ brakes, then reverses. Space brakes.
Q / E glance left / right. Hold C to look behind.
Hold Shift with the pedals for slow, precise control.
Tap steering for fine corrections; hold for a tighter turn.

02 · Follow the lane

Blue arrows lead to your rider. Amber arrows lead to their destination. Stop before red signals. A right turn on red needs a full stop and a clear crossing. Cars use two lanes in each direction.

03 · Pull in neatly

Match the bay’s arrow, center the cab and stop. Watch the parking guide near a bay. Reverse bays need a real backward approach; look behind with C.
Bus: approach each green stop forward, straighten up and hold still until boarding finishes.

04 · Keep the shift going

Earn fares and tips. Each delivery repairs your cab. Three missed calls or an empty condition gauge ends the shift. Choose your starting time and traffic level on the title screen. Fines can put your net balance below zero.
